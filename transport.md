## Aszód EGA: parked bike + train + walking

Destination: **Régész utca 34, Aszód**. Normal first lesson: **07:45**.

| Step | Time / allowance |
|---|---|
| Leave Lelesz utca | **06:40–06:45** |
| Cycle to **Máriabesnyő railway station** | Maps: **5 min**, **1.6–1.8 km**; allow extra rather than relying on that minimum |
| Lock bike and reach platform | Allow **5–8 min** |
| **IR85, train 3012** | **06:59 Máriabesnyő → 07:08 Aszód** |
| Walk from Aszód railway station to EGA | Maps: **25 min / 1.7 km** via Régész utca; alternative **27 min / 1.8 km** via Csengey utca |
| School arrival | **~07:33**, about **12 min before lessons** |
| Door-to-door elapsed time | **~48–53 min** for the departure window above |

The next **IR87, train 542**, runs **07:29 → 07:38**. With the 25-minute walk, it is **too late** for 07:45.

This plan leaves the bicycle in Máriabesnyő; no folding bicycle or bicycle carriage aboard is needed. It is a plausible morning plan, not a trialled commute. Train delays eat into the arrival margin. Bicycle racks, locking arrangements, platform access and the return journey remain unchecked.

### Why the earlier shorter estimates differ

- **28–35 min** in the main research is a **folding-bike estimate**: ~5 min cycling to Máriabesnyő + 9 min train + ~9 min cycling in Aszód + handling/buffer. It is **not** the parked-bike-and-walk option, and its morning feasibility has not been verified.
- Leaving a **second bicycle in Aszód** might shorten the final leg, but parking, theft risk and cycling safety there have not been investigated.

## Aszód EGA: why 413 is not a morning solution

The bus stop **Gödöllő, Máriabesnyő vasúti megállóhely bejárati út** is **not the same boarding location as the railway platform**.

A Maps daytime journey was:

- Walk from Lelesz utca: **27 min / 2 km**.
- **413: 09:16 → 09:27**, to **Aszód, Kossuth Lajos utca 2.**
- Walk to school: **9 min / 550 m**, with uphill/stair sections.
- Whole displayed itinerary: **08:49–09:36 / 47 min**.

Replacing that first walk with cycling suggested **32–35 min**, but that was only a daytime planning estimate—not a usable school-morning itinerary.

The checked 411/413 timetable shows:

| Service | Máriabesnyő bus stop → Aszód Kossuth Lajos utca 2. | School implication |
|---|---|---|
| Earlier **411** | **06:36 → 06:47** | School around **06:56** after walking; nearly 50 min before lessons. |
| First **413** | **09:16 → 09:27** | Too late. |

This does **not** rule out other bus routes; those have not been checked for a suitable morning connection.

## VERKA: parked bike + 455 bus

Destination: **Fő út 117–125, Veresegyház**. Normal first lesson: **08:10**; zero lesson **07:25**.

| Step | Time / allowance |
|---|---|
| Leave Lelesz utca | **Around 07:00** |
| Cycle to **Gödöllő autóbusz-állomás** | **9 min / 3.0 km** via Szent-Györgyi Albert utca; alternative **11 min / 3.4 km** via Fácán sor |
| Lock bicycle / boarding buffer | Allow **~5 min**; remaining time is waiting margin |
| School-day **455** | **07:20 → 07:49 Veresegyház, Fő út 130.** |
| Walk to VERKA | Short walk; school arrival estimated **~07:50–07:55** |
| Door-to-door | **~45–50 min** with a departure around 07:00–07:05; leave earlier for more margin |

This connection is for the **normal 08:10 start**, not the 07:25 zero lesson. The bus leg is timetabled; walking/arrival times are estimates. Bicycle parking/security and return services remain unchecked.

### Other VERKA options

- **Car:** Maps **12.6 km / 23–24 min**, via Dózsa György út. Not a verified school-rush-hour timing.
- **Ordinary public transport:** daytime Maps **63–64 min**, including walking and **405 or local 2 → 455**. Not a verified school-morning connection.
- **Direct cycling:** Maps **12.1–12.3 km / 36–38 min**. Treat this as a routing/time estimate, **not a safe-route recommendation for a 14-year-old**.
- A Szada–Veresegyház cycling segment exists, but a **continuous protected route from Gödöllő to VERKA has not been established**. Congestion and side-street traffic need checking.

## Gábor Dénes, Isaszeg: parked bike + 447 bus

[School research and 2027/2028 admission rules](admissions/isaszeg.md) · [Magyarul](admissions/isaszeg.hu.md) · [Score calculation](scores/isaszeg.md).

**Additional check:** 2026-10-16, using Google Maps in Chrome. Destination: **Gábor Dénes köz 1, Isaszeg**. The 2025/2026 house rules give a normal first lesson at **08:00**, with arrival required **10 minutes before lessons**. Normal zero lesson starts at **07:00**; its frequency remains unchecked.

Maps was set to arrive by **08:00 on Friday, 2026-10-16**:

| Step | Time / allowance |
|---|---|
| Leave Lelesz utca | **~07:08–07:13**, planning allowance |
| Cycle to **Gödöllő, Fürdő utca** bus stop | Maps: **8 min / 2.3 km**, via Őz utca and Fácán sor |
| Lock bicycle and wait | Allow **at least ~5 min**; parking availability/security unverified |
| **447** toward Isaszeg | Maps: **07:28 Fürdő utca → 07:41 Isaszeg, Madách utcai iskola** |
| Walk to school | Maps: **7 min / 500 m** |
| School arrival | **~07:48** |
| Door-to-door | **~35–40 min**, including handling/waiting allowance |

**Important:** arrival is only **2 minutes before the required 07:50 attendance time**. Bus delays can therefore make this connection unsuitable despite arriving before the lesson. This is a Maps itinerary, not an independently reconciled official timetable or a trialled commute. It does not serve a zero lesson.

Other checked Maps results:

- **Ordinary public transport:** **06:59–07:48 / 49 min**, comprising **29 min / 2.2 km walking** to Fürdő utca, the same 447, and the final walk.
- **Train:** **06:51–07:45 / 54 min** with walking at both ends. An earlier detailed itinerary showed **S80 train 3159, 06:42 Máriabesnyő → 06:51 Isaszeg**, followed by **24 min / 1.8 km walking** to school. Replacing the home-side walk with cycling suggests **~40–45 min** with handling/buffer; the later train's precise departure was not separately recorded.
- **Car:** **~15–16 min**; not a trialled school-rush-hour journey.
- **Direct cycling:** **26–28 min / 9.0–9.5 km**. Routing/time estimates only, **not a cycling-safety recommendation**.

Compared with EGA and VERKA, the parked-bike + bus option appears shorter, but **stop parking and the tight arrival margin need resolving**. Return journeys remain unchecked; these results do not establish September 2027 services.

## Still to verify

- Safe cycling routes: crossings, vehicle exposure, lighting and winter conditions—not merely Maps durations.
- Bicycle racks and realistic locking/security at Máriabesnyő station and Gödöllő bus station.
- Actual weekday morning reliability, including traffic and train delays.
- Afternoon return connections and the uphill return cycle from Máriabesnyő.
- Frequency of zero lessons / early attendance requirements.
- Timetables applicable to **2027/2028** before making a final school decision.
