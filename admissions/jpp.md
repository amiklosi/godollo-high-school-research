# Budapest School JPP admission procedure

**Research date:** 2026-09-22  
**Target cycle:** 2027/2028

[Back to the main school research](../README.md)

## Current status

JPP has not yet published its detailed 2027/2028 admission guide or a current open-day date. Hungarian secondary schools must publish their admission guides by **20 October 2026**. The final JPP-specific rules should therefore be checked after that date.

The available evidence shows two parallel processes:

1. the official Hungarian **KIFIR secondary-school application**;
2. BPS’s own **mutual-fit / community-joining process**.

The BPS process is not designed as a conventional points-based oral exam.

## What is currently confirmed

### 1. Submit the BPS interest/application form

The general BPS joining process begins with a web form, advertised as taking about 10–15 minutes. It collects information about the student and family and starts direct contact with the school.

The current BPS application page says that new families can presently be accepted only in the **supporting-contribution category**, subject to available places and the school’s 80/20 funding model.

Application page: <https://opendays.budapestschool.org/jelentkezes/>

### 2. Learn how JPP works

The family is expected to understand BPS’s principles, methods, and distinctive features and to explain why it wants to join the community.

JPP has previously run separate registration-only events:

- student open days with lessons and conversations with current students;
- parent information sessions and forums covering school operation and the admission process;
- additional admission days when places remained.

No current 2027/2028 JPP event was listed on 22 September 2026.

Open days: <https://opendays.budapestschool.org/nyiltnapok/>

### 3. The school gets to know the student and family

The current BPS model requires the learning organizers to determine whether:

- the student, family, and teachers have workable “chemistry”;
- the teachers can support the student’s learning needs;
- the parents understand and can cooperate with the BPS model;
- adding the student will maintain or improve the existing community.

The school explicitly says it wants to know the parents as well as the student. Parent–teacher cooperation is part of the decision.

### 4. The student spends time in the community

The current official model says the student must spend time in the learning community under conditions resembling normal school life as closely as possible. The purpose is for both sides to experience what learning together would be like.

The general joining page describes this as one or more **trial days** or shared programs. JPP may give the event a different name when it publishes the cycle-specific process.

### 5. Fit and group dynamics are assessed

Published BPS criteria include:

- child–parent–teacher dynamics;
- the student’s effect on the existing group;
- age distribution;
- gender balance;
- mix of interests and personalities;
- number and profile of students needing additional support;
- availability in the relevant contribution category.

BPS’s current public explanation of JPP emphasizes:

- ability to work with others;
- learning collaboratively;
- joint problem solving;
- commitment to independent learning.

This means excellent grades help demonstrate academic readiness but do not determine admission.

### 6. Mutual decision and family conversation

The current BPS rules require the consent of the receiving learning community’s organizers. The institution’s decision is community-based rather than made by one principal.

Older JPP material describes a final family conversation in which the school and family review the experience and both decide whether to continue. The current general BPS process similarly describes mutual feedback and a mutual decision.

### 7. Complete the official KIFIR process

JPP participates in KIFIR and published accepted and rejected applicant identifiers in 2026. The BPS-specific process does not replace the official secondary-school application.

For the 2027/2028 cycle:

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| **20 Oct 2026** | Schools publish admission guides |
| **1 Dec 2026** | Deadline to register for the centralized written exam, if taking it |
| **23 Jan 2027, 10:00** | Centralized written exam |
| **12 Feb 2027** | Written-exam result notification deadline |
| **22 Feb 2027** | KIFIR school application deadline |
| **1–19 Mar 2027** | Oral/aptitude period where required |
| **22 Mar 2027** | Provisional rankings |
| **6–8 Apr 2027** | Preference-order modification period |
| **7 May 2027** | Admission/rejection notification |

Although BPS’s general model says it does not require the centralized written exam, taking it remains strongly advisable because the student is also applying to TIG and Szerb, which do require it. The forthcoming JPP guide must clarify exactly how JPP handles the written result in KIFIR.

### 8. Contract and financial onboarding

After mutual acceptance, the family signs a joining contract that creates the student relationship and records the financial contribution.

Currently published JPP figures:

- HUF **228,000/month**, paid for 12 months;
- two-month deposit: HUF **456,000**;
- one additional monthly contribution as an initial school-support fee: HUF **228,000**;
- approximate first-year cash outlay: HUF **3,420,000**, including the deposit.

Confirm the 2027/2028 amount, refund rules, annual increases, and withdrawal notice period before signing.

## Historical JPP/BPS internal progression process

A detailed BPS support article from 2022 described the following process for **students already in BPS grade 8**:

1. self-assessment with the student’s mentor;
2. expression of interest;
3. a project video based on independent research, with at least two weeks to prepare;
4. preparation and presentation of a research topic;
5. a full **DEMO Day** with workshops, presentations, and observation of group dynamics;
6. a family conversation and mutual final decision.

Existing BPS students could sometimes present a portfolio instead of the project task.

This is useful evidence about what JPP values, but it is **old and specifically describes internal BPS progression**. It must not be treated as the confirmed 2027/2028 external-applicant procedure.

## Is there a conventional entrance exam?

The current official BPS model states:

- no required centralized written entrance exam;
- no conventional oral entrance exam;
- no decision based on previous school grades;
- the decision is whether the learning community works better, or at least no worse, with the student and family joining.

In practice, the trial/community day, project or presentation if requested, observations, and family discussion function as a selective assessment. The 2026 public KIFIR list contained **37 accepted and 18 rejected** identifiers, so admission is not automatic.

## How the student should prepare

Do not train rehearsed “correct” answers. Prepare evidence of genuine fit:

1. A concise explanation of why JPP is preferred over a conventional school.
2. Two or three interests the student can discuss with enthusiasm and specificity.
3. One project, piece of work, hobby, or problem the student explored independently.
4. Examples of successful teamwork and a conflict or difficulty handled constructively.
5. A realistic account of how the student organizes work without constant adult direction.
6. Honest strengths, weaknesses, and support needs.
7. Questions for current students and teachers.
8. A small portfolio if available: writing, coding, art, video, research, presentations, competition work, or personal projects.

All-5 grades are a strong signal of diligence, but JPP is likely to care more about whether the student can use that ability independently and collaboratively.

## How the parents should prepare

Parents should be ready to discuss:

- why the BPS model is a deliberate first choice rather than an emergency alternative;
- how much structure versus autonomy the student needs;
- how the family handles feedback and conflict;
- what cooperation with teachers means in practice;
- the daily commute and 9:00–15:10/16:00 schedule;
- the full financial commitment;
- any learning, behavioral, medical, or emotional support needs;
- expectations for Matura and university preparation.

## Questions that still require a direct JPP answer

1. What is the exact 2027/2028 sequence for an external grade-8 applicant?
2. Is there a project video, portfolio, presentation, or DEMO Day this cycle?
3. Is the centralized written exam required, optional, or ignored in ranking?
4. What KIFIR study-area code should be used?
5. How many grade-9 places are available?
6. How many are available in each contribution category?
7. What exactly is observed during the trial/admission day?
8. Who makes the final decision and when is feedback given?
9. Are applicants given individual reasons if rejected?
10. What are the contract, deposit-refund, annual fee increase, and withdrawal terms?

## Recommended immediate action

1. Submit the BPS interest form now or contact JPP so the family is on the notification list.
2. Monitor the JPP and open-days pages after **20 October 2026**.
3. Attend both the student open day and the parent forum.
4. Still register for the centralized written exam by **1 December 2026**, because the other target schools require it.
5. Ask JPP to confirm the complete process in writing before the KIFIR deadline.
