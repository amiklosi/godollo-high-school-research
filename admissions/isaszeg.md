# Gábor Dénes Gimnázium és Technikum, Isaszeg (SZISZI)

**Checked:** 16 October 2026 · **Target:** entry after grade 8 in **2027/2028**.

## Bottom line

**Worth visiting as a potentially less selective, reasonably accessible alternative—not yet an academically equivalent substitute for TIG or Szerb.** The current official page already describes **2027/2028**, with a four-year general gymnasium and a separate five-year IT technikum. Historical admission scores are substantially lower than the researched TIG programs, but lower selectivity does not establish teaching quality or an admission guarantee.

**Important discovery: this is a church-maintained school**, operated by the **Magyar Pünkösdi Egyház** (Hungarian Pentecostal Church), not a secular state-school alternative. The current admission page does not list baptism or a clergy recommendation, but nonreligious-family eligibility and compulsory religious activities need explicit confirmation.

The undated [program page](https://www.sziszi.hu/iskolank/kepzeseink.html) advertises **maximum 26 pupils** for each program. Sulinavigátor records **24 places each in 2026**. **The current admission page does not specify the 2027 place count**; verify KIFIR before applying.

### 0002: general gymnasium

- Conventional preparation for intermediate/advanced Matura and further study, not a BPS-style mentoring/project model.
- Increased language/mathematics hours are conditional on demand: not a separately verified advanced-English track or bilingual program.
- A **2020/2021** supplementary-admission notice listed English first, German second, advanced preparation in years 11–12 and examples such as a Japanese club. These are **historical offerings**, not confirmed 2027 provision.
- Ask for the current weekly timetable, first/second languages, language grouping, advanced-Matura options and actual provision when a group is small.

### 0001: IT technikum

The program page mentions programming, web pages, computer configuration, networking and **Cisco Networking Academy** training/certificates. This is a school description, not independently verified certification success.

The qualification is primarily **systems and application operations**, not a dedicated software-developer qualification. Compare it with TIG 1080 only after considering the extra year, vocational workload, practical placement requirements and the student's actual interests. Confirm the current curriculum, language hours, available hardware, industry partners, qualification/examination timing, financial support and any occupational-health conditions. None of those details should be inferred just from the word “IT”.

## Current 2027/2028 admission system

The official admission page specifies:

```text
School grades          60
Centralized written   100
Oral conversation      40
Maximum total         200
```

Grades counted: **Hungarian grammar, literature, foreign language, mathematics, history and informatics**, at **grade-7 year-end and grade-8 first semester**. Twelve grades, maximum 60: grammar and literature are separate, not averaged together. All relevant 5s give **60/60**, subject to transcript recognition. Confirm that the modern **Digital Culture** subject is accepted as the listed “informatika”, and how BPS combined/report-format subjects are mapped.

The school also says it considers **conduct and diligence**, without publishing a numerical adjustment or tie-break rule on the page. Request the precise rule; do not add invented bonus points.

- A centralized Hungarian/mathematics written exam and **oral conversation** are required.
- No detailed 40-point oral rubric, language task specification or current invitation threshold is published on the checked page.
- SNI/BTMN applications are considered within legal requirements and the school's capabilities. This is **not blanket eligibility for every diagnosis**: arrange any accommodations and confirm support individually.
- Required attachments, tie-breaking and any separate motivation/religious documents need confirmation.

## Religious fit and everyday culture

The current admission page does **not list baptism, clergy recommendations or Pentecostal affiliation** as admission conditions. That absence is not proof that no religious expectations apply.

The **2025/2026 house rules**, section **2.1.9**, refer to participation in church-organised religious instruction **or ethics**, while the behaviour code says religious affiliation is a legally protected personal right. Because these rules cover nursery/primary/secondary units together, they **do not establish the exact grade-9 religious curriculum or an ethics opt-out**. Confirm compulsory lessons, worship, prayers, retreats and any family commitment in writing.

The institution describes itself as quiet, family-like and child-centred; its older publication list describes a **five-hectare green site**. These are **school-reported characteristics**, not independently verified evidence of emotional safety or individual mentoring.

The house rules are recognisably conventional: compulsory attendance, advance notice of major tests, restrictions on phones and appearance, and formal discipline. A small school should not automatically be assumed to offer JPP-style flexibility.

Request the last three gymnasium cohorts' results, advanced-Matura entries, language certificates and destinations, with cohort sizes; keep technikum data separate.

## Recommendation and open-day questions

**Visit 0002 as a potential broader-access fallback**, conditional on student fit, religious expectations, current academic evidence and a workable morning plan. **0001 belongs on the list only if the student actively wants vocational systems/networking**, not merely because its historical admission score is low. No unconditional admission or nonreligious eligibility claim is justified.

Ask:

1. Can an unbaptised, non-practising student attend? Which religious activities are mandatory in grade 9?
2. What are the 2027 places, invitation minimums, oral tasks/rubric, tie-break rules and conduct/diligence treatment?
3. How are BPS grades and Digital Culture mapped to the six admission subjects?
4. Which languages and weekly hours actually run in 0002? How is advanced study supported in small groups?
5. What are the last three **gymnasium-only** cohorts' Matura and university outcomes?
6. Who will teach the next grade 9, and how are bullying, learning difficulties and strong students supported?
7. For 0001: systems vs programming hours, current Cisco opportunities/results, placement partners, qualification timing and all costs?
8. All compulsory/expected payments? Current zero/eighth-period frequency? Which earlier bus do commuters use?
