## Important changes to school choices

### Szerb: 0031 no longer offered

The **2027/2028 guide is published**. The school explicitly states that the **1+4-year language-preparatory program will not start** and is being phased out. **Remove 0031 from the current shortlist.** Its former 69-point written benchmark must not be used as a current fallback.

After grade 8, the advertised option is **0021, four years, 30 planned places**:

- advanced first foreign language: **English or German**;
- second language: English, German, French, Italian, or Latin;
- grade points **50**, centralized written **100**, total **150**;
- **no oral exam** for this four-year track;
- grade subjects/periods unchanged: grade-7 year-end and grade-8 semester results in literature, grammar, mathematics, history, first foreign language;
- where grammar/literature have one combined grade, it is counted twice; where a grade-8 semester result is absent, the grade-7 result is counted twice;
- expected written performance: at least **50%**, not a predicted final cutoff;
- tie-breakers include disadvantaged status and District XVI residence before some other criteria. Matching a historical cutoff alone is not a guarantee.

The historical 0021 written requirements with maximum grades were **76–81/100** in 2023–2026. They remain context only: the intake structure has changed (three six-year classes and only one four-year class).

### EGA Aszód: numerical safety is not eligibility

The **2027/2028 guide was published on 6 October 2026**. It retains **0002 general, 0003 advanced English, 0005 biology**, with the existing **50+100** formula and an additional **40-point oral** for 0003/0005. Those two tracks require at least **90/150 before oral** (40 written with maximum grade points).

**Crucial newly verified requirement:** admission requires **baptism**, with a copy of the baptism certificate/memorial. An unbaptized student can be admitted only by individual permission of the director and a parental declaration committing to baptism **by the end of the first school year**, including congregation/parish membership. The guide also says the school does not accept SNI students under its founding document.

Therefore **EGA 0002 is not an unconditional safety school for a nonreligious family**. Its lower historical cutoff does not bypass this requirement. A genuinely nonreligious backup still needs to be identified if the family cannot accept it.
