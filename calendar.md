# Felvételi calendar — entry in September 2027

**Scope:** grade-8 → secondary-school admission for **2027/2028**, covering TIG, JPP, Szerb Antal, Premontrei, Református Líceum, Aszód EGA, VERKA and **Gábor Dénes, Isaszeg (SZISZI)**.

**Times:** local Hungary time (Europe/Budapest). National dates below are verified against **Annex 1 of the 2026/2027 school-year decree**. School-specific events/status come from the **15 October 2026 research snapshot**, with **Isaszeg added on 16 October 2026**; recheck their linked announcements before attending. Unknown dates are explicitly marked **TBC**.

**No event or exam registrations have been submitted by this research.** Checkboxes below are tasks, not claims that anything has been booked.

## Critical deadlines

- **1 December 2026:** register for the centralized written exam (**központi írásbeli**).
- **23 January 2027, 10:00:** written exam. **Református clergy characterization is also due that day.**
- **22 February 2027:** official school applications / KIFIR submission deadline; BPS may set an earlier internal hand-in date.
- **6–8 April 2027:** preference-order modification window.
- **By 7 May 2027:** schools send admission/rejection decisions.
- **24–25 June 2027:** secondary-school enrolment (**beiratkozás**).

> **Two separate applications:** December registration books the written exam at an exam centre. It does **not** apply to the desired secondary schools. February KIFIR applications are a separate step. JPP's own selection process also does not replace KIFIR.

## January–February 2027: exam, scores and applications

| Date / time | Event or deadline | Action / status |
|---|---|---|
| **22 Jan** | End of grade-8 first semester | Obtain official grades needed for admission-score calculations. The research's all-5 assumption is not a substitute for school-certified records. |
| **23 Jan, 10:00** | **Centralized written exam** | Follow the chosen centre's arrival, identification and permitted-equipment instructions. **Isaszeg asks arrival at 09:00, start 10:00** at the top of its page; its lower summary ambiguously repeats 09:00 as exam time. Confirm centre instructions, not a changed national start. |
| **23 Jan** | **Református Líceum: clergy characterization due** | Current guide specifies personal submission or post. Arrange receipt in advance; a school religious-education teacher's recommendation does not replace it. |
| **By 29 Jan** | Schools notify families of first-semester results | Collect/check the grade-8 semester report and required certified copies. |
| **By 2 Feb, 16:00** | Latest date/time by which centres must provide inspection of marked main-session papers | **Not a universal appointment:** your centre announces its own inspection date. Reserve that date and check the marking against the official key. |
| **3 Feb, 14:00** | Make-up centralized written exam | Only for eligible applicants who missed the original sitting for a justified reason outside their control; **not a score-improvement retake**. |
| **By 11 Feb, 16:00** | Latest date/time for access to marked make-up papers | Check the centre's actual inspection appointment. |
| **By 12 Feb** | Exam centres notify students of written results | Obtain and retain the official evaluation sheet; provide copies where schools require them. |
| **Before 22 Feb; BPS internal date TBC** | Finalize applications and supporting documents | Agree the true preference order; check codes, signatures, certified grades, score sheets, religious documents and school-specific forms. |
| **22 Feb** | **KIFIR application submission deadline** | Primary school forwards application forms to target schools and the student data sheet to OH. Confirm submission with the BPS administrator and save copies. |

**Marking objections / appeals:** their personal deadlines depend on inspection/notification dates and the applicable procedure. Record the exact deadline from the exam centre or decision immediately; do not treat the nationwide inspection cut-offs above as a personal objection deadline.
