# Szerb Antal — score calculation

[Back to score overview](README.md)

## School-grade points

The four- and five-year programs count the end-of-grade-7 and first-semester-grade-8 results in:

- Hungarian literature;
- Hungarian grammar;
- mathematics;
- history;
- foreign language.

With every grade equal to 5:

```text
5 subjects × 2 grading periods × 5 = 50
```

**Result: 50/50 — maximum.**

## Total formula

```text
school grades:      50
centralized exam:  100
oral exam:           0
maximum total:     150
```

| 0021 four-year English | 131 | 81 | 126 | 76 | 131 | 81 | 126 | **76** |
| 0031 English preparatory + 4 | 127 | 77 | 116 | 66 | 127 | 77 | 119 | **69** |

Because there is no oral exam, every extra centralized-exam point directly changes the final total.

Practical targets:

- **0021:** aim for at least **82/100** on the centralized exam;
- **0031:** aim for at least **78/100**.

Those targets clear the recent maximum cutoff by a small margin but do not guarantee admission.
