## 2027/2028 update — 15 October 2026

**0031 is discontinued and cannot be selected this cycle.** The current grade-8 option is **0021**, four years, **30 planned places**, advanced first foreign language English or German. The [current rules](https://szag16.hu/beiskolazas/felveteli-szabalyzatunk) confirm **50 grade + 100 written points, no oral**. Combined grammar/literature grades count twice; a missing grade-8 semester subject result is replaced by the grade-7 year-end result. Historical 0031 figures below are archived context, not a fallback. [Open day and full changes](../admissions/updates-2026-10-15.md).

## School-grade points

The four- and five-year programs count the end-of-grade-7 and first-semester-grade-8 results in:

- Hungarian literature;
- Hungarian grammar;
- mathematics;
- history;
- foreign language.

With every grade equal to 5:

```text
5 subjects × 2 grading periods × 5 = 50
```

**Result: 50/50 — maximum.**

## Total formula

```text
school grades:      50
centralized exam:  100
oral exam:           0
maximum total:     150
```

- **0021:** aim for at least **82/100** on the centralized exam;
