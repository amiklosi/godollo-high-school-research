# TIG vs. Szerb Antal admission chances

[Magyar változat](tig-vs-szerb.hu.md) · [Back to score overview](README.md)

Assumption: maximum school-grade points at both schools, **50/50**.

## Key difference

- **TIG:** centralized written exam plus a 50-point oral exam.
- **Szerb:** centralized written exam only; no oral for the relevant four-/five-year tracks.

Szerb is therefore a useful hedge against a weak TIG oral performance, but generally **not** against a weak centralized written exam.

## 2026 comparison

| Program | Minimum written score implied by the 2026 process | Additional requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| TIG 1010 bilingual | 70 to reach oral stage | written + oral needed 120/150; e.g. written 76 requires oral 44/50 |
| TIG 1020 advanced English | 70 to reach oral stage | written + oral needed 115/150; e.g. written 76 requires oral 39/50 |
| TIG 1080 Digital Culture | 63 to reach oral stage | written + oral needed 106/150; e.g. written 76 requires oral 30/50 |
| Szerb 0021 four-year English | **76** | no oral |
| Szerb 0031 English preparatory | **69** | no oral |

## Interpretation by centralized written score

| Written score | Likely interpretation using recent benchmarks |
|---:|---|
| Below 63 | Neither school is a realistic points-based expectation. |
| 63–68 | TIG Digital Culture oral may be possible; Szerb is generally unlikely, though its prep-year cutoff has occasionally been 66. |
| 69 | Szerb 0031 matched the 2026 cutoff; TIG Digital Culture remains possible. |
| 70–75 | TIG oral invitation is possible for all three tracks; Szerb 0031 is realistic, 0021 generally not. A weak TIG oral could produce a Szerb-only admission. |
| 76–81 | Both Szerb tracks become realistic; TIG still depends strongly on oral performance. This is the clearest range in which he could miss TIG but enter Szerb. |
| 82+ | Strong position for both based on recent years, but TIG still requires a competent oral exam. |

## Example

With a centralized score of **76/100**:

- Szerb 0021 total: 50 + 76 = **126/150**, exactly the 2026 cutoff;
- Szerb 0031 total: **126/150**, seven points above its 2026 cutoff;
- TIG bilingual: needs **44/50** orally to reach 170;
- TIG advanced English: needs **39/50** orally to reach 165;
- TIG Digital Culture: needs **30/50** orally to reach 156.

So yes: a student can miss TIG because of the oral exam and still get into Szerb. If TIG is missed because the centralized exam itself is too low, Szerb is usually not an easier fallback.
