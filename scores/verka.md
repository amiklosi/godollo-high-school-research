## School-grade points

The four-year program uses five subject results from three periods: end of grade 6, end of grade 7, and first semester of grade 8.

1. average of Hungarian grammar and literature;
2. mathematics;
3. history;
4. English for codes 0001/0003 or German for 0002/0004;
5. one chosen subject: biology, geography, chemistry, physics, or Digital Culture.

Formula:

```text
raw sum × 2/3
```

With every grade equal to 5:

```text
5 subject values × 3 periods × 5 = 75
75 × 2/3 = 50
```

**Result: 50/50 — maximum.**

## Total formula

```text
school grades:      50
centralized exam:  100
oral interview:     50
maximum total:     200
```

Recent published benchmarks reported approximately 154–155 points for the English humanities/science tracks. Using 155 as a planning benchmark:

| Written exam | Oral needed to reach 155 |
|---:|---:|
| 55 | 50/50 |
| 60 | 45/50 |
| 70 | 35/50 |
| 80 | 25/50 |
| 90 | 15/50 |

The oral invitation threshold was around 105/150 before the oral in prior years. With 50 grade points, that corresponds to roughly **55/100** on the centralized exam.

The interview also considers community and faith-related experience, so perfect grades do not make admission purely academic.

Source: [official VERKA admission information](https://verka.hu/erdeklodoknek/felveteli-informaciok/).
