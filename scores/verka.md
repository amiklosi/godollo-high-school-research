# VERKA — admission score calculation

**Checked:** 15 October 2026. Formula: official **2026/2027** brochure; **2027/2028 guide still pending**.

[Score overview](README.md) · [School research](../admissions/verka.md) · [Magyarul](../admissions/verka.hu.md)

## School-grade component

Four-year programs count **grade-6 year-end, grade-7 year-end and grade-8 first-semester** results. At each period:

- Hungarian grammar/literature **average**, counted as one component;
- mathematics;
- history;
- English for **0001/0003**, German for **0002/0004**;
- one chosen biology/geography/chemistry/physics/Digital Culture grade.

The brochure FAQ says the chosen science/digital subject need not remain the same across years.

With all relevant grades 5 and the required language grades available:

```text
5 components × 3 periods × 5 = 75
75 × 2/3 = 50
School-grade points: 50/50
```

Do not assume English grades qualify for German tracks. Confirm how BPS combined subjects/reporting are mapped if the transcript differs from the listed subject structure.

## Total

```text
School grades       50
Centralized exam   100
Oral                50
Maximum total      200
```

The oral includes Hungarian and the relevant foreign language; the application includes an approximately one-A4-page **handwritten student introduction letter**. [Preparation details](../admissions/verka.md).

## Historical cutoffs

Source: Sulinavigátor secondary compilation. Each value is a final score **/200**, not the written-exam threshold.

| Track | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0001 humanities / English | 152 | 157 | 154.3 | **155.7** |
| 0002 humanities / German | 143.3 | 146.7 | 148.3 | **157** |
| 0003 science / English | 158 | 157 | 157.3 | **152** |
| 0004 science / German | 142.3 | 153.7 | 131 | **150.3** |

The listed **2026 oral invitation threshold was 100/150 for all four tracks**, implying **50/100 written** with maximum grade points. The previous brochure describes earlier invitation levels as around 105; neither is a fixed 2027 rule.

### Written + oral needed with maximum school grades

| Track | 2026 final cutoff | Written + oral needed | Oral needed if written = 60 | Oral needed if written = 70 | Oral needed if written = 80 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0001 | 155.7 | 105.7 | 45.7 | 35.7 | 25.7 |
| 0002 | 157 | 107 | 47 | 37 | 27 |
| 0003 | 152 | 102 | 42 | 32 | 22 |
| 0004 | 150.3 | 100.3 | 40.3 | 30.3 | 20.3 |

These are arithmetic differences from published rounded cutoffs, **not a claim about attainable oral-score increments**. Equaling a past cutoff does not guarantee admission, including because of tie-breaking.

For the English tracks, **70 written + 40 oral + 50 grades = 160**, above the listed 2023–2026 final cutoffs. **75 + 40 + 50 = 165** adds more margin. These are planning scenarios, not official targets or probability estimates.

## Religious eligibility caveat

Unlike EGA's mandatory baptism/conditional-baptism rule and the clergy-document requirements found elsewhere, VERKA's previous admission FAQ explicitly **does not require a clergy recommendation from families without a close denominational affiliation** who accept the school's values. Its September 2026 pedagogical program also welcomes students without religious practice or denominational ties who are open to Christian values. Two weekly religious-education lessons and a Catholic school culture remain part of the model. Reconfirm **2027/2028** eligibility in writing.
