# Admission score calculations

Assumption: the student received the top Hungarian grade, **5**, in every relevant subject and grading period in grades 5, 6, 7, and the first semester of grade 8.

Most cutoff calculations use the completed 2026/2027 cycle. **[15 October 2026 update](../admissions/updates-2026-10-15.md):** Szerb's 2027/2028 guide confirms the 0021 formula, but **0031 is discontinued**. EGA's new guide confirms its formulas but requires **baptism or an approved commitment to it**; it is not an unconditional nonreligious safety school. TIG's new guide is due 20 October.

## Bottom line

The student has the maximum possible school-grade component everywhere that publishes a numerical grade formula. Admission will therefore depend on:

1. the centralized Hungarian and mathematics exam;
2. oral exams where applicable;
3. faith-related eligibility at Premontrei, Református Líceum, and EGA Aszód;
4. the holistic fit process at BPS JPP;
5. VERKA's Hungarian/foreign-language oral and Christian-value fit. Its previous admission FAQ does not require clergy recommendations from families without close denominational affiliation who accept its values; the September 2026 pedagogical program also welcomes students without religious practice. Reconfirm the new-cycle rules.

6. **Gábor Dénes, Isaszeg:** 40-point oral, conduct/diligence treatment and current invitation minimum need clarification. Pentecostal maintainer; the current admission page lists no baptism/clergy-document requirement, but nonreligious eligibility remains unconfirmed. [Current formula, historical scores and portal-threshold caveats](isaszeg.md).

2026 cutoffs are benchmarks, not guarantees. Applicant strength changes annually.
