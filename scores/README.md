# Admission score calculations

Assumption: the student received the top Hungarian grade, **5**, in every relevant subject and grading period in grades 5, 6, 7, and the first semester of grade 8.

These calculations use the latest publicly available rules, mostly from the completed 2026/2027 cycle. Schools may change formulas for 2027/2028.

## Bottom line

The student has the maximum possible school-grade component everywhere that publishes a numerical grade formula. Admission will therefore depend on:

1. the centralized Hungarian and mathematics exam;
2. oral exams where applicable;
3. faith-related eligibility at Premontrei and Református Líceum;
4. the holistic fit process at BPS JPP.

2026 cutoffs are benchmarks, not guarantees. Applicant strength changes annually.
