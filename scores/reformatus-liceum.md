# Gödöllői Református Líceum — score calculation

[Back to score overview](README.md)

## School-grade points

The four-year program uses end-of-grade-7 and first-semester-grade-8 grades in Hungarian grammar, literature, history, mathematics, foreign language, geography, chemistry, biology, and physics. The published scoring caps this component at 60.

With all relevant grades equal to 5:

**Result: 60/60 — maximum.**

## Total formula

```text
school grades:      60
centralized exam:  100
oral exam:          40
maximum total:     200
```

Scenario totals:

| Centralized exam | Before oral | With 20/40 oral | With 30/40 oral | With 40/40 oral |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 60 | 120 | 140 | 150 | 160 |
| 70 | 130 | 150 | 160 | 170 |
| 80 | 140 | 160 | 170 | 180 |
| 90 | 150 | 170 | 180 | 190 |

## Critical non-score requirement

The 2026/2027 guide says a minister’s characterization is required. The school may reject a student who does not meet its religious expectations or does not submit that document. Students are invited to the oral only if the characterization arrived by the deadline. Perfect grades do not override this requirement.

Source: [2026/2027 admission guide](https://grl.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Felveteli-tajekoztato-2025_2026.pdf).
