# Budapest School JPP — admission calculation

[Back to score overview](README.md)

BPS JPP does not publish a conventional formula that converts school grades into admission points.

**Result: no numerical school-grade score to calculate.**

All 5s are a positive academic signal, but the school says admission emphasizes:

- collaboration;
- learning with others;
- joint problem solving;
- commitment to independent learning;
- fit between the student, family, and school model.

The centralized written exam is not presented as the main admission criterion. Previous processes included project or presentation work, a group day, and a family conversation. The current process should be requested directly from JPP.

Sources: [JPP official page](https://opendays.budapestschool.org/micro-schools/jpp), [2026 admission-results page](https://opendays.budapestschool.org/oldalak/kifir-felveteli/).
