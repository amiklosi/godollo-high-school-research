## School-grade component

Both announced programs—**0002 four-year gymnasium**, **0001 five-year IT technikum**—use the published school-wide formula:

- **Grade-7 year-end and grade-8 first semester**;
- Hungarian grammar, literature, foreign language, mathematics, history and informatics;
- grammar and literature count **separately**, not as an average.

```text
6 subjects × 2 periods × 5 = 60
School-grade points: 60/60

School grades          60
Centralized written   100
Oral conversation      40
Maximum total         200
```

This is an all-5 planning assumption, not a fresh audit of the student's reports. Confirm **Digital Culture → informatics** equivalence, treatment of BPS combined subjects, the required foreign-language grade, and any missing/exempt grades. The school also considers conduct/diligence but the page does not provide their precise numerical or tie-break treatment.
