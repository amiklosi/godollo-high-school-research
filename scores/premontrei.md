# Premontrei Szent Norbert — score calculation

[Back to score overview](README.md)

## School-grade points

The four-year program counts results from the end of grade 7 and the first semester of grade 8 in:

- mathematics;
- history;
- Hungarian grammar and literature;
- foreign language;
- biology;
- geography;
- chemistry;
- physics;
- informatics/Digital Culture.

With all relevant grades equal to 5, the student receives the published maximum:

**Result: 100/100 — maximum.**

## Total formula

```text
school grades:                    100
centralized exam × 2:             200
language oral:                     50
faith-related oral interview:      50
maximum total:                    400
```

| 70/100 | 140 | 240 | 290–340 |

No reliable final cutoff was established in this research, so these are totals rather than admission predictions.

## Critical non-score requirement

Perfect grades do **not** remove the religious-admission barrier:

- clergy recommendation is a mandatory document under the published process;
- the motivation letter must describe religious practice and church ties;
- the faith interview is worth 50 points;
- the pedagogical program says the school expects baptized, practicing applicants.

Before treating this as a realistic choice, obtain written confirmation that a nonreligious application will be accepted and ask whether anything may replace the clergy recommendation.
