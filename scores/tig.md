## School-grade points

The 2026 formula was ten times the average of the relevant non-skills subjects from the end of grades 5, 6, and 7 and the first semester of grade 8.

With every relevant grade equal to 5:

```text
average = 5.00
school-grade points = 5.00 × 10 = 50
```

**Result: 50/50 — maximum.**

## Total formula

```text
school grades:       50
centralized exam:   100
oral exam:           50
maximum total:      200
```
